## Library Card

A library card at Ton Duc Thang University is the user's student, lecturer, or staff card.

- Location: Room A0003, Building A, HCMC Campus
- Telephone: (028) 37 755 054

### Postgraduate Students

- Contact: Department of Graduate Studies
- Location: Room B002, Building B, HCMC Campus
- Telephone: (028) 37 755 059
- Email: gradstudies@tdtu.edu.vn

### Lecturers and Staff

- Contact: INSPiRE Library
- Location: Building G, HCMC Campus
- Telephone: (028) 37 755 057
- Email: library@tdtu.edu.vn

### Important Note

Users should complete the Library Orientation requirements before their library card is activated.

## Library Portal Account

- The Library Portal Account is the same as the user's Student Information Portal account or Lecturer/Staff Information Portal account.
- Users who do not have the corresponding university portal account should contact the appropriate department for support.

### Undergraduate Students

- Contact: Student Services Office
- Location: Room E0001, Building E, HCMC Campus
- Telephone: (028) 2247.7215
- Email: tuvanhocduong@tdtu.edu.vn

### Postgraduate Students

- Contact: Department of Graduate Studies
- Location: Room B002, Building B, HCMC Campus
- Telephone: (028) 37 755 059
- Email: gradstudies@tdtu.edu.vn

### Lecturers and Staff

- If a lecturer or staff member already has a Faculty/Staff Information Portal account but does not yet have a Library Information Portal account, contact INSPiRE Library.
- INSPiRE Library is located in Building G, HCMC Campus.
- Telephone: (028) 37 755 057
- Email: library@tdtu.edu.vn
- If a lecturer or staff member does not have a Faculty/Staff Information Portal account, contact the Department for Personnel & Administrative Affairs.
- Location: Room A0009, Building A
- Telephone: (028) 37 755 035
- Email: phongtchc@tdtu.edu.vn
