# Undergraduate Student - Circulation Service

## Loan Periods

### Non-circulating Materials

- Maximum items: 2
- Loan period: 1 day, within the building's opening hours
- Renewal: Not available

### Circulating Materials

- Maximum items: 2
- Loan period: 5 days
- Maximum renewal: 1 time, for an additional 5 days
## Renewal

### Renewal Criteria

- The item must currently be on loan.
- The item must not have been requested by another user.
- A successful renewal extends the loan period by 5 days.
- Overdue items are not eligible for renewal.

### Renewal Methods

- Users can renew borrowed items online through the Library Information Portal.

## Fines and Lost Items

### Overdue Fines

- Fine rate: 20,000 VND per day per item.

### Policy Violations

- After the third violation in a year, users will receive a library notice of admonishment.
- More than three violations in a year will be referred to the Department of Student Affairs and the Department of Inspection and Internal Supervision for further action.

### Lost and Repair Fees

- For books still in circulation: Replace the original item and pay a handling fee of 20,000 VND.
- For out-of-print books: Compensation is three times the book's list price at the time of compensation, adjusted for inflation, plus a handling fee of 20,000 VND and a shipping fee equal to 15% of the book's list price.
- For the loss of one volume in a multi-volume set: Compensation is five times the list price of the entire set divided by the number of volumes, adjusted for inflation, plus a handling fee of 20,000 VND and a shipping fee equal to 15% of the book's list price.
- For rare materials: Replace the original item, or compensate ten times the item's value if the original cannot be obtained, plus a handling fee of 20,000 VND and a shipping fee equal to 15% of the item's list price.

## Material Reservation

### Reservation Policy

- The reservation service allows users to place a hold on materials that are currently checked out by another user.
- Reservations apply only to materials that are currently borrowed by another user.

### How to Place a Reservation

1. Search for and select the desired material on the Library Information Portal.
2. Open the `Get It` tab.
3. Click the `Hold` button.
4. Check the return date and the number of existing holds.
5. Click `Send Request` to submit the hold request.
6. Monitor your campus email for a notification when the material is ready for pickup.
7. Visit the Service Desk on the 1st Floor to collect the reserved material.

### Important Notes

- Reservations are only available for materials currently checked out by other users.
- Reservations do not apply to materials that are currently available in the library or designated for onsite use.
- Users should check their campus email for pickup notifications.
- Reserved materials are held at the Service Desk on the 1st Floor for 1 day.
- If the material is not collected within 1 day, it will be made available to the next user in line or returned to general circulation.

## Borrower's Responsibilities

- Borrowers must properly care for borrowed materials and are responsible for any loss or damage.
- Borrowers should not lend their Library Card to others or exchange borrowed materials with other users.
- If a Library Card is lost, borrowers must inform the relevant department to obtain a replacement.
- Borrowers should check the due date to ensure timely returns. The Library also sends reminder notifications through campus email.
- In special cases, the Library may request that borrowed materials be returned earlier than the original due date.
